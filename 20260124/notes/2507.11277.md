# Taming Uncertainty via Automation: Observing, Analyzing, and Optimizing Agentic AI Systems

**Citation / Link:** arXiv:2507.11277v1  
**Authors:** Dany Moshkovich; Sergey Zeltyn  
**Year:** 2025  

---

## 1. One-Line Contribution
Introduces an AgentOps framework and automation pipeline for observing, analyzing, optimizing, and automating operations of agentic AI systems to manage uncertainty.

---

## 2. Problem the Paper Solves
Agentic AI systems are non-deterministic, adaptive, and tool- and memory-driven, making traditional observability and operations insufficient for reliable deployment and continuous improvement.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution / Architecture
The paper defines AgentOps as a framework tailored to agentic AI and proposes a six-stage AgentOps Automation Pipeline: observe behavior, collect metrics, detect issues, identify root causes, optimize recommendations, and automate operations. It maps needs across four roles (developers, testers, SREs, business users), surveys the observability/analytics landscape, and details categories of signals, failure types, and optimization actions to support automated, self-improving operations.

---

## 4. Key Concepts Introduced
- AgentOps framework for agentic system operations
- AgentOps Automation Pipeline (six stages)
- Role-specific observability and analytics needs

---

## 5. Relation to AI Agents / Agent Governance
Provides an operational governance framework for monitoring, diagnosing, and automating improvements in agentic systems, emphasizing safety, reliability, and business-aligned tradeoffs.

---

## 6. Strengths
- Clear lifecycle pipeline linking observability to automated remediation
- Explicit role-based framing that ties technical signals to organizational needs
